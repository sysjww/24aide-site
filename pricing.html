01 / TOTAL COST

Know what is included—and what is extra.

One-time setup

Essential $300 · Professional $500 · Premier $800. A 50% deposit starts work; the remaining 50% is due at go-live.

Includes phone-number provisioning work, AI call-flow configuration, business hours and service-area settings, phone connection setup, and testing across agreed scenarios. Recurring number rental or carrier charges are separate where applicable.

Choose call forwarding from your existing business number, a dedicated number, or time-based routing such as after-hours coverage. The connection method does not change the tier price.

Usage and additional charges

  • Voice above the included allowance: $0.30 per minute.
  • Messages above the included allowance: $0.03 per message. Essential has no bundled message allowance; its text-back messages are billed at this rate.
  • Unused included usage does not roll over. Annual billing does not pool monthly allowances.
  • Phone-number rental, carrier fees, premium integrations, custom development, and applicable taxes are additional where applicable and disclosed in the written order before approval.

Before activation, the Order Form states how minutes and messages are counted, the reporting and monthly allowance cycle, and any usage alerts or spending limits. No unapproved add-on is activated.

02 / OPERATING STANDARD

Automation with defined boundaries.

Availability and oversight

All tiers are designed for 24/7 AI answering. The monthly uptime target is 99.5%, excluding planned maintenance and events outside our reasonable control. This is a performance target, not a guarantee of uninterrupted service or 24/7 human staffing.

Planned maintenance carries 48-hour notice; urgent security or restoration work may require shorter notice. Scripts, service areas, schedules, transfer destinations, integrations, and reporting recipients are confirmed before launch.

Consent, safety, and data

Texts, follow-ups, AI voice use, and recordings are enabled only with the required consent, notices, identification, and opt-outs. An unanswered call does not by itself authorize marketing outreach.

Urgent calls follow your escalation rules. The service does not diagnose hazards, promise emergency assistance, or replace emergency services. You retain your business data, with a CSV data export on termination under the agreed transition plan.

Advertising management, website development, and legal advice are not included.

Approve first. Stay in control.

For annual prepayment, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current monthly service interval. Remaining full unused months are refunded at the discounted monthly equivalent, without clawing back the discount. Setup, service failures, and other exceptions follow the Refund Policy.

Before paid work begins, your Order Form confirms the selected plan, actual total, billing dates, integrations, approved extras, usage measurement, and implementation assumptions. 04 / BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few practical answers.

Do I need to replace my business number?

No. You can forward your existing number, use a dedicated service number, or route calls only during selected hours. Number compatibility and any carrier charges are confirmed before approval.

Are these prices for real estate businesses too?

No. CraftCall AI plans cover home service reception workflows. Real estate AI systems have different workflows and integrations and are quoted separately after a free audit. Start the real estate audit.

What happens if usage exceeds the plan?

Overage rates are $0.30 per voice minute and $0.03 per message above the included allowance. Measurement rules, reporting, and any agreed alerts or spending limits are documented before activation. You can ask about a higher tier before increasing your usage.

Can I buy directly from this page?

This page is for comparison and pre-contract review. Contact us to confirm the configuration and receive an Order Form. Clicking a contact link or submitting a website form does not start billing.
