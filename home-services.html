Before you ask.

Do I have to change my phone number?
No. Most shops keep their number and forward unanswered calls to the AI. A dedicated line or after-hours-only routing also works — it's a setup choice, not a plan difference.
What happens at 2 AM?
The AI answers, takes the details, and texts you a summary. Urgent keywords on the Pro plan call you immediately; everything else waits for morning.
Does it work with my scheduling tools?
It connects to the calendar and job tools named in your scope — confirmed during the audit before you approve anything.
Is this just an answering service?
An answering service takes a message. This one texts the caller back, books the job, sends reminders, recovers no-shows, and reports what moved.
Who sees my customer data?
You do. Your business data and conversation transcripts remain yours, with a documented export path.
