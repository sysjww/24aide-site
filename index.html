The system covers the moments generic automation misses: portal competition, open-house volume, and the weekend gap.

06 / SYSTEM SCOPE

A defined job for each stage of lead response.

The system is configured around your channels, approved information, and handoff rules. It handles repetitive response work; agents keep control of advice, negotiation, and exceptions.

INTAKE

Acknowledge configured inquiry channels

Calls, forms, texts, or portal inquiries in scope receive an approved first reply when the connected channel is available.

MISSED CALLS

Open a text conversation after a missed call

When the phone integration records a missed call, the workflow can send a pre-approved text and ask what prompted the call.

QUALIFICATION

Collect the details your agents need

Property, timeline, budget range, financing stage, and viewing preference can be gathered before the handoff.

SCHEDULING

Offer only verified availability

The workflow reads the approved calendar rules, presents eligible times, and records confirmations or rescheduling requests.

FOLLOW-UP

Run a documented contact cadence

Messages continue on the schedule you approve, with stop conditions for opt-outs, human takeover, and closed conversations.

CRM

Revisit older records with context

Reactivation starts only after the records, prior relationship, and permitted channel have been reviewed.

REVIEWS

Send review requests after approved events

The request can be triggered after a confirmed milestone rather than relying on an agent to remember it later.

OVERSIGHT

Show what happened and what needs attention

Reporting summarizes response times, conversations, appointments, escalations, and items that still require a person.

Where the boundary sits

The system does not replace licensed judgment.It does not invent listing facts or availability.It does not make unsupervised promises to a lead.Its job is to acknowledge, gather, route, schedule within approved rules, and preserve context for the agent.

08 / DECISION QUESTIONS

What to know before requesting the audit.

Clear answers about the system, the working relationship, and the boundaries around AI.

What will the free audit tell me?

The report traces each inquiry channel in scope, records the observed response and handoff path, identifies evidence gaps, and ranks practical fixes. It also shows the assumptions behind any opportunity estimate, so you can challenge or replace them.

When would an AI lead-response system make sense?

A build is worth considering when the audit finds repetitive delays that clear rules and connected tools can address—for example, after-hours acknowledgment, basic qualification, verified scheduling, or follow-up reminders. If the main problem is ownership or notification settings, the report recommends that simpler fix instead.

Which parts of a real-estate conversation stay with an agent?

Advice, negotiation, exceptions, sensitive situations, and any fact the system cannot verify route to a person. The automated scope is limited to approved information, approved questions, scheduling rules, and documented handoffs.

Can the system work with our current phone, CRM, and calendar?

Often, but compatibility is confirmed during scoping rather than assumed. The build proposal identifies each integration, the permissions it needs, and any limitation before you approve the work.

Can it follow up with older CRM contacts?

Only after the records, prior context, channel rules, and opt-out history have been reviewed. Reactivation is segmented and bounded; it is not a blanket message to every historical record.

How are mistakes and uncertain answers handled?

The workflow uses approved sources and explicit escalation rules. Unverified listing facts, unavailable calendar data, or questions outside scope are handed to the designated person instead of being guessed.

How is pricing decided?

The audit is free. If you ask us to scope a build, the quote separates the one-time implementation from the monthly managed-service plan and identifies expected third-party costs. Nothing is built until you approve that written scope.

Is a discovery call required?

No. The intake, clarification, and audit report can be handled through the form and email. A call is optional if a workflow or integration would be easier to clarify live.

Will my team need to operate the automation?

Your team needs to own the business decisions and human escalations, but not the underlying automation. We document the handoff points and keep the operating view focused on conversations, appointments, exceptions, and approvals.

What happens after the report?

You can implement the recommendations internally, share them with another provider, or request a separate build scope from 24aide. If a system is launched, ongoing monitoring and tuning are covered by the managed-service plan you approve.
