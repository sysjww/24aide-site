08 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Reasonable questions. Straight answers.

AI should not get a free pass with your customers. It should earn a narrow, supervised role.

Can I trust AI to talk to my leads?

Not unsupervised and not about everything. We define the questions it may answer, the details it must collect, and the moments it must hand off. The goal is a fast, useful first response—not an artificial expert pretending to know what it does not.

Do I need to take a sales call?

No. The process is asynchronous: intake form, email follow-up if needed, then a written report. If you later want a live call, we can discuss options, but the audit does not depend on one.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. There is no fee for the initial audit. If there is a worthwhile system to build, you will receive a custom scope and quote separately. You keep the report whether or not you continue.

What does it cost after the audit?

The audit is free. If the gaps are worth fixing, you receive a custom build quote — a one-time install plus a monthly operate plan, sized to your lead volume. You approve the numbers before anything is built. No long-term contracts required.

What if the AI says something wrong?

We design around that risk rather than ignore it. The system uses approved knowledge, explicit guardrails, escalation rules, transcript review, and narrow permissions. High-risk or uncertain questions route to a human instead of inviting a guess.

I am not technical. Will I have to run this?

No coding is expected from your team. We map the workflow in plain English, build the system, document the handoffs, and keep the operational view focused on conversations, appointments, and exceptions.

How do we work together?

Fully asynchronous by default: intake form → email → written report. No discovery call required. If a live conversation would help later, it can be arranged.
