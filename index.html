01 / WHERE IT LEAKS

A lead can disappear at three predictable moments.

First comes the unstaffed hour, then the shrinking response window, then the follow-up that has no clear owner.

06:52 PM
AFTER HOURS

The lead arrives after the office closes.

Buyer timing rarely matches office hours. If the first useful reply waits until morning, the same prospect may already be speaking with another agent who answered that night.

Example: A Zillow inquiry arrives at 9:14 PM on Saturday. The buyer is still looking, comparing listings, and ready to talk — but the office is not.

00:05
THE FIRST FIVE MINUTES

The active-search window is short.

A useful reply delivered while the buyer is still searching can preserve the conversation. An hour later, the same reply may land after another agent has already taken the next step.

↻
THE FOLLOW-THROUGH

The quiet leak starts after the first reply.

A lead can receive a good opening response and still fade. Reminders, review requests, and longer follow-up sequences are easy to postpone when a team's day fills up, so this loss often stays invisible.

02 / WHAT YOUR AI RUNS

One system that never clocks out — and does all of it.

Not separate apps you stitch together. One AI system, trained on your process, running your front-of-house and the follow-through at the same time.

ANSWERING

Conversations covered around the clock

Configured calls, texts, and forms receive an approved first response even when the office is closed or the team is occupied.

SPEED-TO-LEAD

Follow-up starts when the lead arrives

New inquiries enter the agreed contact sequence immediately and continue until the prospect books, declines, opts out, or needs a person.

RECOVERY

Missed calls become text conversations

When an included phone line logs a missed call, a pre-approved text, where legally permitted, opens the conversation and captures why the prospect called.

INTAKE

Qualification with routing rules

The workflow asks the questions your team selected, records the answers, and sends each conversation to the right next step.

BOOKING

Showings booked to verified calendars

Eligible times come from the connected calendar, with confirmations and rescheduling handled inside the same conversation.

RETENTION

Reminders and no-show recovery

Scheduled nudges reduce forgotten showings and give missed appointments a defined path back to the team.

REPUTATION

Review requests after key milestones

Once your approved closing event is recorded, the system sends the request that busy agents often remember too late.

FOLLOW-THROUGH

Reports that keep ownership visible

Conversation summaries, next actions, and exceptions show the team what moved, what stalled, and who needs to step in.

07 / FAQ

What people ask before the audit.

Straight answers about the service, the system, and where people stay in control.

What does 24aide actually do?

We first trace how inquiries enter, move, and stall across your real estate operation. If the findings support a build, we connect an approved response and follow-up workflow to the tools already in use, then monitor that workflow after launch. Its job is to reduce delay, preserve context, and put the right conversations in front of an agent.

Is this just an AI answering service?

No. An initial reply is only the first step. The configured workflow can collect context, route the lead, offer verified times, send reminders, recover missed appointments, request reviews, and show the team which conversations still need human action. The audit determines which of those jobs belong in your scope.

How is this different from a chatbot?

A typical chatbot sits on a page and reacts to questions. This system coordinates a defined sequence across the channels and tools in scope — from the first message through scheduling, follow-up, reporting, and agent escalation.

What does it cost?

The real estate audit is free. Real estate implementation and ongoing operation are quoted separately after the review, before you commit. Home service businesses can review our published CraftCall AI plans. Choose your service and pricing path.

Do I have to replace my current tools?

Usually not. We begin with the phone, CRM, calendar, inbox, and other tools already supporting your process. The scope identifies any compatibility limit before you approve the installation.

Does a human stay in the loop?

Yes. Your team authorizes the information, language, questions, scheduling rules, and escalation triggers. Negotiation, advice, exceptions, sensitive matters, and unverified facts are sent to a designated person with the conversation context preserved.

Can I trust AI to talk to my leads?

Trust comes from limits and review, not from assuming the model is always right. Before launch, the system is tested against realistic situations and restricted to approved sources and actions. It must escalate when listing details, availability, promises, or consequential questions cannot be verified, and its live behavior remains subject to monitoring.
