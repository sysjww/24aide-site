Refund Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy applies to CraftCall AI, a monthly software subscription sold by 24aide ("we", "us") and billed through Paddle, our merchant of record. It explains how cancellations and refunds work.

Cancelling your subscription

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time by emailing hello@24aide.com.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Your service remains active until that date, and you will not be charged again after it.

Subscription fees

  • Monthly subscription fees are billed in advance for each billing period.
  • Fees for the current billing period are non-refundable. If you cancel mid-period, the subscription runs to the end of that period and no partial refund is issued for the remaining days.

One-time setup fees

  • If you cancel before setup work on your account has begun, your setup fee is refunded in full.
  • Once setup work has begun, the setup fee is non-refundable, because configuration and testing work has already been performed.

Exceptions

  • If a technical failure on our side makes the service unusable for a sustained period and we cannot restore it, we will refund the affected period's subscription fee.
  • Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection law.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued through Paddle to your original payment method. Processing times depend on your bank or card issuer, typically 5–10 business days after approval.

Contact

For cancellation or refund requests, email hello@24aide.com from the email address on your account.
